# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Event guests and walk-up customers use this on a shared, full-screen Windows kiosk in bright, distracting environments. They may be unfamiliar with the booth, may be standing with friends, and need to complete the entire flow in about a minute without staff assistance.

## Product Purpose

Thermal Photobooth turns a short webcam session into a tactile 58mm monochrome photo strip and sends it directly to a locally attached JK-5802H printer. Success means a guest can choose a layout, follow automatic countdowns, approve the generated receipt, print once, and reset safely for the next person.

## Brand Personality

Playful, tactile, and confident. The product should feel like a real physical machine: immediate, legible, celebratory, and slightly nostalgic without becoming themed or childish.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, glassmorphism, neon cyberpunk camera interfaces, scrapbook decoration, fake paper textures, and tiny control-dense photo editors. Do not resemble a social network or ask users to manage files and accounts.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action unmistakable from several feet away.
- Let the machine lead the rhythm through automatic capture and concise status language.
- Preview the physical result honestly at the 58mm receipt proportion.
- Keep editing reversible, limited, and subordinate to printing.
- Recover gracefully from denied camera access, disconnected printers, and abandoned sessions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use high contrast, large touch targets, visible keyboard focus, semantic status announcements, and instructions that do not rely on color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep countdown information both visual and textual.
